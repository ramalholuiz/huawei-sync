A sync utility that behaves like a product.

Huawei Sync moves health & fitness data from Huawei Health into Android Health Connect, then out to every app that reads from it — GymRats, Strava, Google Fit, Samsung Health, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura. This canvas is ten screens plus ten ideas. Tap the sync button, ask the assistant a question, flip the theme.

Ten things Health Sync does not do

Ten UX ideas that would make this the best sync app on the platform.

Small, shippable, most of them already visible somewhere in the ten screens above. None of them exist in Health Sync today.

01
READABLE PIPELINE
Show the sync engine, don't hide it.
A live pipeline screen that names each stage — Read, Validate, Transform, Write — with per-stage latency and per-destination status. Users trust what they can see.
02
PROACTIVE ASSISTANT
An AI that reads the logs.
"Why didn't today's run reach GymRats?" gets a real answer, not a help article. The model sees your data, your destinations, and the last 30 days of sync history.
03
FIX, NOT ERROR
Every diagnostic ends in a button.
Error codes are banned from the surface. Each issue: what happened, why, one-tap fix (deep-linked to system settings when needed). No copy-and-paste to a support page.
04
REPLAY A DAY
Re-fire yesterday's queue.
If a destination app was offline or missed a webhook, replay pushes just those items. No re-syncing months of history to fix one gap.
05
RULES AS SENTENCES
Automation, not toggle lists.
Every rule reads as English. "After every workout." "Only on Wi-Fi." "Sync less on weekends." No screens of nested checkboxes.
06
LEARNED RULES
Rules the app writes for you.
The app watches your usage and proposes automations. "You never open Fit on weekends — sync every 2 hours instead of every 30 minutes?" You approve, it applies.
07
PER-ACTIVITY DESTINATIONS
Each workout knows where it lives.
The activity screen shows which apps received THIS run and which are still queued — plus an inline Push button on the ones that failed. No hunting through logs.
08
MAPPING TRANSPARENCY
Show the schema mismatches.
"Skiing" doesn't exist in Strava, so we log it as "Alpine Ski". The app tells you, once, per mapping. No silent data drift.
09
SYNC AS THEATRE
Every sync has a face.
Tap Sync Now and every destination is visible in the animation — you can literally watch your run leave Huawei and land in each app. Progress with meaning.
10
CENTRE OF GRAVITY
A dashboard, not a settings page.
The home screen is a live status of your health data and its destinations. Settings is the smallest tab, not the whole app. Sync stops being a chore.
